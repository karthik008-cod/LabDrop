How to Share Lab Files to Your Phone in Seconds

3 simple steps • No login required • Auto-expires in 30 mins
Instructions

LabDrop is designed for students, programmers, and researchers who need to share lab files to phone without signing into personal email, Google Drive, or WhatsApp on public college computers.

⚡ Step 1

Drop Files or Code

Drag & drop lab assignments, documents, or paste code snippets directly into the workbench.

📱 Step 2

Scan Instant QR Code

A secure temporary QR code is generated instantly. Point your phone camera or QR scanner to open the transfer.

🔒 Step 3

Download & Auto-Expire

Download individual files or full ZIPs straight to your mobile phone. Files auto-delete after 30 minutes.

Why LabDrop (labdrop.online)?

Unlike LANDrop, AirDrop, or Nearby Share which require installing dedicated desktop applications and companion mobile apps or require both devices to be on identical subnets, LabDrop operates 100% in your browser at labdrop.online with zero software installation, zero driver configuration, and zero personal accounts.
